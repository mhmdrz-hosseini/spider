# Channel to Persian Brand Skill

Reverse-engineer high-performing foreign short-form video channels at a macro level and convert their retention mechanics into an original Persian brand identity and content funnel.

---

## When to Use

- Adapting English or foreign video channels (e.g., SwoloTips) for the Iranian/Persian market.
- Transitioning from copying single reels to reverse-engineering a full-channel content system.
- Designing a localized mascot, visual universe, and monetization funnel.

---

## 3-Phase Execution Workflow

### Phase 1: Macro Channel Audit

Analyze sample videos across the reference channel as an integrated system:

1. **Content Tiers**: Classify posts into Tier 1 (Viral reach/format series), Tier 2 (Authority/myth-busting), and Tier 3 (Product/app conversion).
2. **Retention Skeleton (Keep 100%)**:
   - *Hook Syntax*: Exact formula (e.g., "What happens if you do X for 30 days?").
   - *Pacing*: 4-part milestones (Week 1 to 4) with visual pattern interrupts every 2–4 seconds.
   - *Layout*: Safe zones, kinetic subtitles (1–2 words), and minimal UI interference.
   - *Closing Mechanics*: Assumptive follow commands or comment-to-DM automated triggers.
3. **Visual Skin & Metaphor Engine (Replace 100%)**:
   - Character design, color temperature, and emotional style.
   - Mechanical/anatomical overlays vs. anthropomorphized character gags.
4. **Audio & Pacing**: Voiceover cadence, script density, and sound effect accents.

---

### Phase 2: The Strategic Grill (Interactive Discovery)

Before drafting content, question the user across 5 strategic pillars (3–5 focused questions per round):

1. **Monetization Endgame**: What is the core business model (mobile app, digital product, coaching, physical goods)? How does the mascot embed into the product UI?
2. **Target Persona & Local Friction**: Who is the Iranian audience? What daily habits, misconceptions, or cultural pain points apply?
3. **Opposite-Emotion Visual Pivot**: How should the visual identity flip from the reference (e.g., moving from dark/stoic green to warm/expressive blue) to establish proprietary IP?
4. **Cultural Anchoring**: What Iranian foods, environments (*Sofreh*, commute, home rituals), idioms, and local products replace generic Western tropes?
5. **Production Realities**: What is the production stack (Blender, AI keyframe-to-video like Kling/Runway, motion graphics)? What is the target weekly output?

*Rule: Challenge generic answers and offer 2–3 creative directions during the grill.*

---

### Phase 3: Brand Identity & Strategy Blueprint

Synthesize findings into four core deliverables:

1. **Brand Bible & Mascot Sheet**:
   - 3–5 Persian brand names with phonetic appeal.
   - Palette hex codes (Background, Mascot, Accents, Subtitles).
   - Character spec (proportions, expressions, signature items, sidekick).
   - Locked master prompts for 3D/AI generation.
   - Subtitle typography, safe-zone specs, and chapter cards.
2. **Cultural Metaphor Matrix**:
   - Comparison table mapping reference tropes to Iranian cultural equivalents.
   - Anthropomorphized internal concepts (e.g., organs/muscles as relatable characters).
3. **Macro Content Funnel**:
   - *Tier 1 (70%)*: Repeatable viral series with assumptive follow CTAs.
   - *Tier 2 (20%)*: Iranian market myth-busting and trending topics.
   - *Tier 3 (10%)*: Friction-to-solution product demos using comment-to-DM triggers.
4. **Production Pipeline & Pilot Storyboard**:
   - Step-by-step production checklist.
   - 1 complete pilot storyboard table (Timecode, Persian Script, Subtitle, Visual/Metaphor Prompt, Audio/SFX).

---

## Core Guidelines

- **Skeleton vs. Skin**: Never duplicate the original visual identity. Retain the proven pacing and hook skeleton, but completely redesign the visual skin and tone.
- **Localize Concepts, Not Words**: Avoid direct translation. Ground every script in natural, colloquial Persian and recognizable daily life situations.
- **Match Production Feasibility**: Align visual metaphor complexity with the creator's actual tools and output cadence.
